title: Concepts
description: How @nqlib/nqgantt splits engine and Gantt UI.
---

nqgantt splits **engine** (schedule math, `@nqlib/nqgantt-engine`) from **bar UI** (`@nqlib/nqgantt`). Your app owns tasks, dependencies, and persistence.

| Layer | Package |
|-------|---------|
| Schedule math | `@nqlib/nqgantt-engine` |
| Bars, ports, edges | `@nqlib/nqgantt` |
| Host | Your task list, save/load, product defaults |

For board views over the same work set, see [nqgrid](/docs/nqgrid). For surrounding chrome, see [nqui](/docs/nqui).
