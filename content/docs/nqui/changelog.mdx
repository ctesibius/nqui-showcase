## 0.9.1 — 2026-11-07

- Filled `Button` and `Badge` get their top sheen back: `--button-gradient` is a white sheen
  again instead of `none`. Set it to `none` for the flat look.
- Half-star `Rating` no longer shows a seam down the middle of the star.

## 0.9.0 and 0.8.3 — 2026-11-04

**Slider** grew from one control into a family, by props:

- `label`, `showValue`, `formatValue` — the value sits at the end of the label row.
- `scale="log"` and `marks` — ratio values (100 % mid-track on 25–400 %), snapping to labelled marks.
- `variant="field"` (type an exact value), `variant="segments"` (5–12 steps) and
  `variant="ruler"` (ticks that adapt to width, `tickIntervals` for units).
- `origin` (bipolar fill), `trackBackground` + `showRange` (gradient tracks) and `readOnly`.

Slider, Switch and Progress moved onto shared control tokens (`--track`, `--thumb`,
`--shadow-thumb`), and filled buttons press to a solid darker fill. See the
[Slider lab](/recipes/sliders) and [Forms and fields](/docs/nqui/forms-and-fields).
